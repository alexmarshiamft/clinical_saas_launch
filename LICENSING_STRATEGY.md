## 2. In-Depth Analysis of Licensing Models

### Model 1: White-Label OEM Master License (Recommended Strategy)
- **Mechanism**: You license the entire TheraFlow OS frontend, workflow engines, and PostgreSQL schema to an established digital health company or EHR vendor under a Master OEM Software License Agreement.
- **Why It Eliminates Liability**:
  - The licensee hosts the application on their own HIPAA-compliant cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure).
  - The licensee executes Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) directly with clinicians and enterprise practices.
  - The licensee provides customer support, handles billing disputes, and manages clinical incident response.
  - Your agreement contains clear "as-is" software warranties with a comprehensive liability waiver and licensee indemnification for clinical or regulatory claims.
- **Financial Structure**:
  - **Upfront Implementation / Handoff Fee**: **$100,000 – $150,000** due upon signing and delivery of the repository.
  - **Ongoing License Royalty**:
    - Option A: **$7,500 – $15,000/month flat platform fee**, or
    - Option B: **$25 – $40 per active clinician seat per month** deployed by the licensee (e.g., 500 clinician seats = $15,000/month recurring).

### Model 2: Source-Code Buyout with Retained Residual IP
- **Mechanism**: The buyer acquires a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use, modify, sub-license, and rebrand the source code, while you retain the right to use the core algorithmic libraries for non-competing projects.
- **Financial Structure**:
  - Single lump-sum payment of **$250,000 – $375,000** with 30 days of transitional technical handoff assistance (up to 20 hours of Zoom architecture walkthroughs).

### Model 3: Exclusive Vertical License (e.g., Addiction Treatment / CMHC)
- **Mechanism**: Grant an exclusive license restricted to a single non-competing clinical niche (e.g., Substance Use Disorder clinics or Pediatric Speech Therapy), reserving the right to sell or license the platform into General Adult Outpatient EHRs or Telehealth networks.
- **Financial Structure**:
  - **$85,000 – $120,000** upfront per vertical + 15% royalty on incremental ARR generated by the module.

## 3. Recommended Licensing Terms & Protective Guardrails

To ensure you never incur SaaS or healthcare operational liabilities, any licensing contract must include the following mandatory clauses:

1. **Strict Non-Operating Stance**:
   - *"Licensor is a software developer and intellectual property licensor only. Licensor does not operate, manage, host, or administer a clinical service, telehealth platform, electronic health record, medical billing service, payroll tax filing agency, or chartered banking institution."*
2. **Licensee BAA & Regulatory Assumption**:
   - *"Licensee acknowledges and agrees that Licensee shall act as the Covered Entity or Business Associate (as defined under HIPAA) with respect to all end-user deployments, patient interactions, and Protected Health Information (PHI). Licensee shall execute all required BAAs and maintain all administrative, physical, and technical safeguards."*
3. **Banking & Payroll Non-Liability Clause**:
   - *"Licensor provides provider abstraction interfaces and sandbox simulation software only. Licensor is not a money transmitter, custodian of customer funds, or chartered depository institution. Licensee shall be solely responsible for securing direct partner contracts with regulated Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) and payroll tax filing providers (e.g., Gusto, ADP, Unit, Stripe Treasury)."*
4. **No Direct Clinician Support**:
   - *"Licensor shall have no obligation to provide tier-1 or tier-2 support to end-user clinicians or patients. All customer service, onboarding, and training shall be solely the responsibility of Licensee."*
5. **Comprehensive As-Is Warranty & Disclaimer**:
   - *"The software is provided 'as is' without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular clinical purpose, or non-infringement. Licensor shall not be liable for any medical diagnostic errors, billing claim denials, payroll calculation discrepancies, or data breaches occurring on Licensee's infrastructure."*

## 4. Final Recommendation: Sale vs. Licensing Decision Matrix

- **If an outright acquirer offers $\ge \$175,000$ cash at closing**:  
  **Take the outright sale**. It provides an immediate clean break, requires zero ongoing relationship management, and yields guaranteed liquidity.
- **If acquirer offers are below $\$125,000$ or if prospective partners already have substantial customer distribution**:  
  **Execute a White-Label OEM Master License**. Secure $\$75,000$ upfront plus $\$5,000-\$10,000$/month recurring royalties. This yields $\$135,000–\$195,000$ in Year 1 while preserving the underlying IP asset.
